How it worksFour honest steps

1

Find change

Brightness is matched on the pixels that changed least, then any pixel whose colour difference beats the scene's own noise (median + k·MAD) is marked.

2

Read shape

Machines cut straight, simple edges; nature spreads irregularly. Fractal dimension, rectangularity and solidity score each patch.

3

Read context

A new thin track leading to a clearing, water that was already there, a lease boundary it crosses — each adds or subtracts points.

4

Seal it

Every step goes into a SHA-256 chain and a fingerprinted report, so nobody can quietly edit the result later.

Limits we state up front

  • Labels are "likely" and meant for triage — deciding where to look first.
  • On RGB-only images, water is a colour proxy that misses turbid or weed-covered water; a 5-band GeoTIFF adds NDVI / MNDWI physics.
  • Images are not co-registered, and seasonal crop changes show up as change.
  • Hashes prove nothing was altered after analysis. They do not prove where or when an image was captured.
